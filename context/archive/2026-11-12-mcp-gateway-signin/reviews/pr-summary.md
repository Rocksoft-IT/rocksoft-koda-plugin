**What this delivers:** Rocksoft Koda now signs clients in with their Microsoft
work account instead of asking them to type and confirm an e-mail. Once
connected, the assistant reads the client's repositories and creates issues
automatically for the signed-in account — nothing else about the conversation
changes. If an organization is not yet approved for access, or sign-in has not
happened yet, the client now sees a clear message telling them what to do
instead of a confusing error.

**Status:** Ready to merge; nothing to check by hand. Note: it only works
end to end once the sign-in gateway and the matching workflow update on the
Rocksoft side are live — until then, clients should stay on the current
version.
